1. **Narrative Summary**
   - The child described getting a puppy named Pepper together with their mother and sister Ella. The puppy licked the child’s face when they met her and was wriggly. The child held the puppy, which made them feel nervous due to its movement. Afterward, the puppy sat on the child's knee all day and fell asleep in its new bed at the end of the day.

2. **Communication Strengths**
   - The child demonstrated clear and organized storytelling skills by describing a sequence of events: getting the puppy (getting her name), interactions with the puppy (licking, holding), and the conclusion (sleeping).
   - The child used specific details about Pepper's appearance (black and white) and behavior (eating at the knee).

3. **Communication Gaps**
   - The child had trouble providing more information on where they got the puppy.
   - The child did not provide additional details or feelings about owning a puppy other than excitement.

4. **Emotional Understanding**
   - The child was able to describe their feelings and how the events made them feel, such as excitement and happiness.

5. **Recommended Future Lesson Focus**
   - This session focused on describing past events with specific details. In future sessions, the child can be encouraged to elaborate more about various aspects of their day (e.g., what they did before or after getting the puppy).
   
6. **Suggested Reachy Prompts**
   - *Simple*: "Can you tell me about something exciting that happened today?"
   - *Natural*: "Do you remember when we talked about the puppy? How did she look and act?"
   - *Child-Friendly*: "What fun things did you do with your new puppy Pepper?"
