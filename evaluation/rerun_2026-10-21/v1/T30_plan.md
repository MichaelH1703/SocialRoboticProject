### 1. Narrative Summary

- The child describes attending her auntie Shona's wedding at a real castle with a tower. They were accompanied by Uncle Dev, cousins, mother, father, and many other people. She wore a purple sparkly dress with a bow on the back and shiny shoes that were slightly too big.
- At night, they went home in a taxi where she fell asleep.

### 2. Communication Strengths

- The child demonstrated understanding of complex concepts such as sparklers, fireworks, real castles, and the difference between big numbers like "loads" and "loads."
- She correctly repeated important details about her dress, including the colors and design.
- Her recall of events during the day indicates good episodic memory.

### 3. Communication Gaps

- The child had difficulty recalling specific details about what happened at the wedding ("I don't remember").
- She used a simplified version to answer therapist's questions rather than providing more complex or detailed information.

### 4. Emotional Understanding

- The child expressed that she felt very excited when waking up for her cousin Leo’s birthday (at age four) and described him as a baby.
- When asked about the best day ever, she responded with enthusiasm and used phrases like "the best ever ever."

### 5. Recommended Future Lesson Focus

- Continue working on enhancing recall skills using strategies such as visualization techniques or breaking down tasks into smaller parts.
- Practice recounting stories in greater detail to help with memory recall and comprehension.

### 6. Suggested Reachy Prompts (simple, natural, child-friendly)

1. **"Show me the picture of your sparkly dress at the wedding."**
2. **"Can you tell Uncle Dev about the castle again?"**
3. **"How did it feel to be in a taxi after the wedding? Was it exciting or scary?"**
4. **"Remember when you fell asleep in the taxi? What happened next?"**
5. **"What were some of your favorite parts from Auntie Shona's wedding?"**
