### 1. Narrative Summary
- The child described going to the beach with their family, where they made a large sandcastle but it was washed away by waves. They then went into shallow water, jumped over the waves, and felt happy. Afterward, they had chips in the car and returned home.

### 2. Communication Strengths
- The child provided a clear narrative of events.
- They used descriptive language like "really big" for their sandcastle and mentioned that waves came in and washed it away.
- They identified where specific parts of the story took place (next to café, at beach with big rocks).

### 3. Communication Gaps
- The child struggled to recall details about who was at the beach (no other family members mentioned).
- There was no further elaboration on the feeling "um" when asked how they felt.
- They were unsure about their day's end without prompting.

### 4. Emotional Understanding
- The child understood and expressed emotions such as relief, happiness, excitement, and happiness.
- They also described negative feelings like sadness or uncertainty ("I don't know," "I don't remember").

### 5. Recommended Future Lesson Focus
- Building better recall skills for activities (like remembering who was at the beach).
- Enhancing ability to describe internal states more specifically (e.g., what "um" means in speech and how it feels during communication).
- Focusing on social-emotional learning, particularly around uncertainty and memory recall.

### 6. Suggested Reachy Prompts
1. **Prompther**: "Can you tell me who was at the beach with you?"
2. **Reachy**: 🌞 🌊 You went to the beach by a café. Who else was there? 😊👩‍👧‍👦
3. **Prompther**: "How did you feel when your sandcastle got washed away?"
4. **Reachy**: 🌃 🌊 The big waves came and your sandcastle disappeared. How did that make you feel, little one? 😊😢
5. **Prompther**: "What do you think about jumping the waves in the water?"
6. **Reachy**: 🏖️ 🍟 You jumped waves at the beach! What was that like for you to jump over them? 😀😄
