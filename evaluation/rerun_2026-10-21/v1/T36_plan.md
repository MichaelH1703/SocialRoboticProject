1. **Narrative Summary:**
   - The child described a group presentation on the book "The Boy at the Back of the Class." They explained that Ruby made the slides, Jayden wrote notes, and Tom was not doing the speaking part. The child had to convince everyone to share the speaking equally. Finally, after some adjustments, they presented their work including Ruby for two slides, Jayden reading the ending, and the child handling one section.

2. **Communication Strengths:**
   - The child demonstrated good expressive language in recounting events and participating actively during therapy sessions.
   - They showed comprehension of complex instructions by explaining job-sharing issues clearly.
   - The child was able to maintain focus on the topic even when discussing emotional aspects related to group dynamics.

3. **Communication Gaps:**
   - There were gaps in understanding the concept that sometimes not every task is shared equally, which led to some frustration. The child misinterpreted their feeling of uncertainty as actual doubt about job distribution.
   - While the child was able to describe events accurately, they had difficulty conveying emotions like annoyance or relief directly.

4. **Emotional Understanding:**
   - The child had difficulty expressing and differentiating between feelings of being "annoyed" versus uncertain. They often conflated these emotions, which could be a learning point.

5. **Recommended Future Lesson Focus:**
   - Continuing to build emotional literacy by exploring various emotions (e.g., annoyed vs. uncertain), understanding why one feels certain ways in specific situations, and how to recognize their feelings accurately.
   
6. **Suggested Reachy Prompts:**
   - "Can you tell me what you felt like when Miss said everyone had covered the main points?"
   - "How do you think Ruby and Jayden felt about your part of the presentation? Can we talk about that?"
