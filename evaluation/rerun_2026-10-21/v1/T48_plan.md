### 1. Narrative Summary

The child describes having lunch at the canteen with friends Alfie and Ben. They waited in line before it was moved to a different location due to a dinner lady's instruction. The child, along with their friends, worked their way through the tables and eventually got served pasta. They noticed that there were trays on the floor which made navigation difficult.

### 2. Communication Strengths

- The child accurately described where they waited initially and where it was moved to.
- They provided specific details about who else was present (Alfie and Ben) and what happened when they arrived at the new queue location.
- The child communicated their feelings about not knowing where to go.

### 3. Communication Gaps

- The child struggled with explaining their anxiety or frustration effectively, leaving it ambiguous whether they were anxious or just confused about the change in queue location.
- There was little detail provided about how Alfie and Ben were situated initially (whether they were already at a table) or what happened after lunch.

### 4. Emotional Understanding

The child expressed feelings of frustration and uncertainty, especially regarding the change in queue locations. They felt anxious when there was no clear communication from staff about the shift.

### 5. Recommended Future Lesson Focus

- **Understanding Change Management**: Discuss different scenarios where things might change (like the canteen's queue) and how to manage those changes effectively.
- **Communicating Emotions**: Teach children how to describe their emotions more clearly, such as using phrases like "I felt confused" or "I was anxious."
- **Following Instructions**: Practice understanding instructions from adults in different situations.

### 6. Suggested Reachy Prompts

1. **Simple Prompt for Understanding Changes**:
   - "Tell me what happens when you have to wait in line and things change."

2. **Prompt for Clear Expression of Emotions**:
   - "How did you feel when the canteen changed the queue?"

3. **Prompt for Practicing Following Instructions**:
   - "Imagine you're at a new place. What should you do if you don't know where to go?"
   
These prompts are designed to help both Reece and other children better understand different scenarios, express emotions clearly, and follow instructions more effectively.
