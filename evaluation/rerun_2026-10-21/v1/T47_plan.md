### 1. Narrative Summary
The child described attending a concert by Olivia Rodrigo at the O2 in London with her older sister. They went through several steps including queuing for security, finding their seats, enjoying the music, adjusting to lighting changes, and even going home via the Tube.

### 2. Communication Strengths
- The child was able to provide clear details about their concert experience.
- They accurately described various aspects of the event, such as the venue, queueing process, seat location, audio experiences (especially feeling the bass), and visual experiences during the show.
- The child mentioned singing along with specific songs, indicating some engagement and familiarity with the music.

### 3. Communication Gaps
- The child did not provide information about what they enjoyed or disliked specifically about the concert experience, except for how difficult it was at one point due to rapid flashing lights.
- There were no details given about interactions with other attendees or how the concert staff interacted during the event.

### 4. Emotional Understanding
The child expressed feeling happy and excited about attending the concert but also noted that some parts of the experience, such as waiting for security and adjusting to sudden lighting changes, caused discomfort or overwhelm.

### 5. Recommended Future Lesson Focus
For future sessions, we should focus on expanding the child's storytelling by encouraging them to share more about their emotional responses and reactions during different parts of the concert. Additionally, helping them describe specific interactions with other attendees or staff can provide a richer understanding of their experience.

### 6. Suggested Reachy Prompts (simple, natural, child-friendly)
- "Can you tell me what song your sister knew you wanted to hear?"
- "What did you like about waiting for security? What didn't feel good about it?"
- "Who do you think the people at the ticket desk were talking to? How did they make you feel when you approached them?"

These prompts will help facilitate a more in-depth exploration of Priya's concert experience, enhancing both her communication skills and emotional understanding.
