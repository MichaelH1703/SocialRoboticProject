### 1. Narrative Summary

The child described a visit to their cousin's place where they played Mario Kart with the auntie and Lewis. They encountered an issue with the Wi-Fi during gameplay which led to a game freeze, causing frustration because they were on the last lap with the blue shell ready to use. Lewis then used his phone hotspot to reconnect, allowing them to continue playing until it was time for them to go home.

### 2. Communication Strengths

- The child provided clear and detailed information about their day.
- They managed to describe a multi-step event sequence accurately.
- The child demonstrated an understanding of the game's rules (e.g., using the blue shell correctly).
- The child used expressive language ("annoying" when describing the frustration).

### 3. Communication Gaps

- There was some difficulty in conveying the exact timing and sequence of events during gameplay, particularly regarding the freeze at the last lap.
- Less elaboration on Lewis's actions (e.g., how he connected the hotspot).
- The child did not mention any other activities they might have done with their cousins apart from playing Mario Kart.

### 4. Emotional Understanding

The child was able to describe their emotional state accurately when explaining that the game freeze was frustrating because they were winning. They used expressive language ("annoying") and gave a clear explanation of why it was frustrating, showing an understanding of the context and significance of their emotions.

### 5. Recommended Future Lesson Focus

Given the child's strengths but acknowledging areas for improvement, future lessons could focus on enhancing narrative sequencing, especially regarding event timing details like game freezes and how they affect gameplay progress. Additionally, lessons should concentrate on elaborating more on contextual information and providing additional examples to support their accounts.

### 6. Suggested Reachy Prompts

1. "Can you tell me what happened after the game froze? What did Lewis do?"
2. "Can you describe in more detail how winning feels like for a Mario Kart player?"
3. "Tell me about what you and your cousin(s) did after playing Mario Kart. Did you play any other games together?"
4. "When something unexpected happens in a game, how does it make you feel? Can you tell us why?"
