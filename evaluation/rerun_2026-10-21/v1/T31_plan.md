1. **Describe Your Feelings**: "Can you tell me how it felt when everyone started talking over the fire alarm? Did that make you feel calm or did your ears start to hurt?"
2. **Relate to a Familiar Situation**: "When you were lined up by the fence outside, did you like waiting in line with other kids? How did you stay quiet and still?" 
3. **Problem Solving**: "What if the fire alarm went off again next time at school? Can we think of some things that might help make it easier for everyone to listen?"
4. **Rehearse Safe Behavior**: "If there is ever a loud noise like the fire alarm, what can you do to stay safe and wait quietly until it stops?"

These prompts aim to facilitate communication about emotions, encourage social skills practice related to noisy situations, promote problem-solving techniques useful in stressful scenarios, and reinforce safety measures.
