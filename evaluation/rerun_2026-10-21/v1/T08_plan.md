1. **Narrative Summary**
   - The child described dropping their phone at the beginning of the day and then falling off a scooter on a path outside their house. They went fast down the hill, hit a stone they didn’t see, fell on their knee, which bled, got a plaster, and returned home.

2. **Communication Strengths**
   - The child was able to narrate the events in sequence using basic language.
   - They correctly identified the cause of their injury (not seeing a stone) and described its consequence (bleeding).

3. **Communication Gaps**
   - The child struggled with descriptive details, such as not recalling specific words or phrases like "path" when describing the setting.
   - There was an inconsistency in tense between past events ("fell... bleeding") and future actions ("got a plaster").

4. **Emotional Understanding**
   - The child could describe their knee being red, indicating they recognized physical changes due to injury.

5. **Recommended Future Lesson Focus**
   - Continue working on the sequence of events narration.
   - Enhance the child’s ability to provide descriptive details and use different tenses in storytelling.
   - Improve understanding and expression of emotions, such as fear or pain, through more interactive activities like drawing or using props.

6. **Suggested Reachy Prompts**
   - "Tell me about the path where you fell."
   - "What did your knee look like when it was bleeding?"
   - "Can you show me how you felt after falling?"
   - "Imagine if a stone wasn’t there, what do you think would have happened? Let’s pretend together."
   - "How does your knee feel now?"
