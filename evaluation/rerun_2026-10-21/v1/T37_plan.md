### 1. Narrative Summary

The child described a trip to Birmingham with their uncle involving a train journey from Coventry to Birmingham International station. The display at the platform initially indicated Platform 4, but they were actually on Platform 3 when arriving. They went through barriers to reach Birmingham New Street where they visited a model railway shop and found a Hornby locomotive that was £89.99, which is cheaper than some limited editions. They had lunch (a cheese sandwich and water) and took the 15:42 train back to Coventry.

### 2. Communication Strengths

- **Narrative Continuity:** The child clearly related a chronological narrative of their day's events.
- **Use of Specific Details:** The child provided details such as exact times, station names, and specific locomotives seen.
- **Logical Flow:** There was a logical sequence to the trip (train journey → model railway visit → lunch → return train).

### 3. Communication Gaps

- **Incomplete Response on Train Type:** When asked what type of train returned, the child only corrected by saying "Voyager," not indicating any emotional response or additional context.
- **Lack of Emotional Language:** The child did not use words that conveyed feelings such as disappointment or excitement about the locomotive purchase.

### 4. Emotional Understanding

The child expressed some understanding and knowledge about trains, specifically mentioning the type (Hornby locomotives) they saw at the model railway shop. They showed disappointment when realizing they couldn't buy the locomotive but didn't express strong emotions such as joy or frustration in a way that could be observed.

### 5. Recommended Future Lesson Focus

- **Handling Emotions:** Help the child develop emotional vocabulary and learn how to articulate their feelings (e.g., using words like "disappointed" or "excited").
- **Logical Sequencing:** Work on reinforcing the sequence of events in a narrative.
- **Attention to Details:** Practice providing specific details about train types, prices, etc.

### 6. Suggested Reachy Prompts

1. *"Can you tell me how you felt when your uncle said you couldn't buy the locomotive?"*
2. *"What did you do after visiting the model railway shop? Did anything else happen then?"*
3. *"Describe what happened before and after you saw the Hornby locomotives. How were those moments different from each other?"*

These prompts help reinforce understanding of emotions, sequencing events, and providing specific details while supporting future interactions with a robot assistant named Reachy.
