1. **Narrative Summary**
   - The child described going to a big Tesco with their mother where the mother left them briefly while they went shopping elsewhere. The child got lost but felt wobbly in their tummy and was crying. A woman with a green badge found them and told everyone that the child (the narrator) was at the front by the tills. The mother then ran to find her child, giving them a big hug. They ended up getting face cakes as rewards for good behavior.

2. **Communication Strengths**
   - The child demonstrated an ability to recall specific details about their experience with Tesco and the events that occurred.
   - The child was able to use abstract concepts like feeling wobbly in their tummy effectively.
   - The child displayed a clear understanding of social interactions, recognizing when the mother wasn’t around by using emotional cues (wobbly stomach).

3. **Communication Gaps**
   - The child had difficulty providing more descriptive details about what they saw or did during the time they were alone at Tesco.
   - There was a lack of specific instruction provided to Reachy regarding how to interact with children who have experienced separation anxiety.

4. **Emotional Understanding**
   - The child demonstrated an understanding and expression of emotions such as being wobbly in their tummy, feeling sad or scared (from not seeing their mother), and happy after receiving a hug.
   - The child was also able to convey feelings of relief when they found themselves back with their mother.

5. **Recommended Future Lesson Focus**
   - Focusing on strategies for managing separation anxiety and providing more opportunities for the child to practice social interactions, specifically ones where they can interact positively with adults who have a green badge.
   - Teaching coping mechanisms like deep breathing or using visual aids (e.g., drawing pictures of themselves being alone) to help them understand what it feels like when they are alone.

6. **Suggested Reachy Prompts**
   - "Can we draw how you felt when mommy was gone?"
   - "What does a green badge look like? Can we find one in the room?"
   - "Remember when you were scared but then saw mommy and felt happy again. How did it feel when that happened?"
   - "Let’s act out what you did at Tesco, pretending to be someone with a badge or your mom."
   - "Can you tell me more about how you felt inside the store?"
