1. **Narrative Summary**
   - The child describes making a snowman with her father and sister Ava. They played a snowball fight, leading to the father falling over in the snow. Mr Snowy (the snowman) melted the next day due to sunlight. The story ends with describing a polar bear.

2. **Communication Strengths**
   - The child is able to recall and describe events in sequential order.
   - She uses spatial concepts like "bigger" and "littler."
   - The child demonstrates understanding of physical states (e.g., "up to here") and objects ("Mr Snowy").
   - Her communication shows emotional engagement through describing the height of snow on her boots.

3. **Communication Gaps**
   - There is a confusion about who melted: Mr Snowy or Dad.
   - The child's vocabulary could be expanded, particularly for more complex tasks like understanding that sunlight causes melting.
   - The child’s language could benefit from more precise descriptions (e.g., instead of "bigger and bigger" she might say "first we made a big ball then a small one").

4. **Emotional Understanding**
   - The child shows emotional engagement through her responses, particularly when describing the height of snow on her boots and how Mr Snowy's transformation happened.

5. **Recommended Future Lesson Focus**
   - Continue work on sequencing events in time.
   - Reinforce spatial concepts such as "bigger" and "smaller."
   - Expand vocabulary related to physical states (e.g., melting, shrinking).
   - Enhance understanding of cause-and-effect relationships like sunlight causing snowman to melt.
