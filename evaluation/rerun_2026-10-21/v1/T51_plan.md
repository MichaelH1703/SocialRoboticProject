1. **Narrative Summary**
   - The child described volunteering at the charity shop where they were introduced to a till system, clothing tags, and basic stock management tasks. They also learned about customer service, such as finding items not on display or in storage.

2. **Communication Strengths**
   - The child was able to recall specific details from their first day of volunteering, including the manager’s instructions, questions asked by customers, and steps taken during the shift.
   - The child demonstrated problem-solving skills by asking for help when they couldn't find a coat and showing initiative in organizing donated books.

3. **Communication Gaps**
   - The child had difficulty expressing feelings or emotions explicitly. They mentioned being worried but were not clear about their exact concerns (feeling responsible vs. feeling unsure about the procedure).
   - There was limited use of past tense verbs ("were working with" instead of "we worked with") and simple explanations, which could be improved for more structured and consistent communication.

4. **Emotional Understanding**
   - The child showed some emotional recognition but needed clarification on specific emotions related to their experiences (worry vs. responsibility). They had a positive initial attitude ("It was okay").

5. **Recommended Future Lesson Focus**
   - Continued work on expressive language skills, particularly in describing feelings and thoughts more explicitly.
   - Emphasizing the use of past tense verbs for clearer expression and structure.
   - Strengthening problem-solving strategies by providing more opportunities to practice with real-life situations.

6. **Suggested Reachy Prompts**
   - "Can you tell me about how you felt during your volunteering? Were there any worries?"
   - "What did Denise show you when you couldn't find the coat?"
