### 1. Narrative Summary

The child described attending their auntie's wedding with family members including mom, dad, brother, Auntie Saira, and many relatives. The event took place at a hotel near Leicester, where they had to wait in a room with bright lights and gold chairs filled with perfume. They felt overwhelmed by the sensory information and went outside for air. During the meal, their cousin kept tapping his glass with a spoon, which annoyed them but not enough to throw anything out of the window. The child watched the dancing from their seat until it was time to leave before having cake.

### 2. Communication Strengths

- **Engagement**: The child actively participated in the conversation by providing details about the wedding and using descriptive language.
- **Organization**: They organized their thoughts logically, starting with what happened upon arrival, followed by meal-time experiences, dance events, and leaving at the end of the day.
- **Use of Specific Details**: Mentioned specific sensory experiences like perfume and cleaning spray smells.

### 3. Communication Gaps

- **Vocabulary Use**: Used "horrible" for describing their experience but could have used more precise language to describe how they felt (e.g., overwhelmed, distracted).
- **Complex Sentences Construction**: Some responses were simple statements rather than complex sentences that incorporated multiple ideas.
- **Emotional Expression**: The child did not express feelings with intensity or in depth. They mentioned wanting to throw the spoon but didn't explain why it was frustrating or angry.

### 4. Emotional Understanding

The child understood and could describe their emotions at various points during the day, like feeling uncomfortable (horrible), annoyed by the cousin’s tapping glass, and leaving early because of school commitments. However, they did not use detailed language to express feelings in a nuanced way.

### 5. Recommended Future Lesson Focus

- **Improved Vocabulary**: Work on expanding vocabulary related to emotional states such as happy, sad, confused, and frustrated.
- **Complex Sentence Structure**: Teach ways to construct complex sentences using conjunctions like "but" or "however," for example, instead of simple statements.
- **Sensory Descriptions**: Enhance their ability to describe sensory experiences more vividly.

### 6. Suggested Reachy Prompts

1. *Narrative Prompt*: "Can you tell me what happened when we went outside for a bit?"
2. *Vocabulary Prompt*: "Tell mommy and daddy how you felt inside the room."
3. *Descriptive Prompt*: "What did the room smell like? Can you describe it more?"

These prompts are simple, natural, and designed to encourage both verbal and sensory descriptions, which can be easily adapted for robot interactions.
