### 1. Narrative Summary

The child described a trip to the cinema with their cousin Ethan where they saw the new Spider-Man film. They mentioned queuing up, watching the trailers, finding it frightening due to loud noises, someone checking their phone during the movie, and staying for an extra scene after the credits.

### 2. Communication Strengths

- The child provided clear details about different parts of their trip.
- They used sequential language (e.g., "We got tickets on the machine... We stayed until the credits finished") to organize events in time order.
- They demonstrated basic recall skills by accurately recounting various aspects of the day, such as what happened during and after the film.

### 3. Communication Gaps

- The child sometimes provided incomplete or vague information (e.g., "Someone kept checking their phone" without specifying whose phone).
- There was an inconsistency in when events occurred ("We got tickets on the machine... We stayed until the credits finished"), leading to some confusion.
- They did not provide explicit details about the content of the film, such as describing parts of specific scenes or characters.

### 4. Emotional Understanding

The child expressed their experience both positively and negatively:
- Positive aspects: They found it better than they expected ("Better than I expected") and enjoyed a particular scene ("The bit with the train was good").
- Negative aspects: They felt frightened by loud noises during the film and uncomfortable with someone checking their phone.

### 5. Recommended Future Lesson Focus

Future lessons should focus on enhancing sequential narration, improving detail description, and refining emotional expression in communication. Specifically:

1. **Sequential Narration**: Encourage the child to speak more clearly about events occurring in chronological order.
2. **Detail Description**: Practice providing specific examples of what happened during different parts of their day at the cinema.
3. **Emotional Expression**: Work on expressing emotions more clearly and specifically, helping them describe how they felt differently throughout various parts of the trip.

### 6. Suggested Reachy Prompts

1. "Tell me about when you got tickets for the Spider-Man movie."
2. "What did Ethan do while you waited in line?"
3. "Describe one part of the trailer that was really loud and scary."
4. "How did it make you feel to watch someone on their phone during the film?"
5. "Why didn't you say anything to that person?"
6. "Can you remember a funny or interesting moment from the movie?"

These prompts are simple, child-friendly, and aim to support both structured therapist-led sessions and interactions with the robot (Reachy).
