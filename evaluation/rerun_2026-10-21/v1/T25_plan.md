1. **Narrative Summary**
   The child described a situation where all the lights went out at home on Wednesday night after dinner. This caused chaos as they had to navigate around in the dark without a functioning torch. Eventually, they found candles and played "I Spy" by candlelight. They liked the part of playing "I Spy" best.

2. **Communication Strengths**
   - The child was able to provide specific details about their experience.
   - The child showed understanding of safety measures (e.g., not touching hot objects).
   - The child demonstrated the ability to describe feelings and emotions, such as their heart racing in the dark.
   - The child could retell a complex story from beginning to end.

3. **Communication Gaps**
   - There was some difficulty with sequencing events clearly (e.g., when did it happen? How did they get out of the bath?).
   - The child had trouble expressing emotions more fully, such as fear or frustration.
   - The child needed more practice in retelling stories in a cohesive manner.

4. **Emotional Understanding**
   - The child demonstrated an understanding of their emotional state (heart racing fast) and the safety measures needed to deal with dangerous situations.

5. **Recommended Future Lesson Focus**
   - Continue practicing sequencing events clearly, especially when there are multiple steps involved.
   - Work on expanding emotional vocabulary and describing emotions more vividly.
   - Practice retelling stories in a cohesive manner, emphasizing beginning-middle-end structure.

6. **Suggested Reachy Prompts (simple, child-friendly)**
   - "Can you tell me the story of what happened again?"
   - "What did you feel like when it was dark?"
   - "Let's practice telling someone about your day and using 'I' statements."
   - "How can we make our stories more clear and organized?"
