1. **Narrative Summary:**
   - The child described making cookies with their grandma at her house. They mixed the dough and waited while it was in the oven because Grandma thought they were good at that task. After cooling, they ate one each cookie and brought some home when Mum came to get them.

2. **Communication Strengths:**
   - The child provided a clear account of making cookies with their grandma.
   - They mentioned details like using flour, butter, and chocolate chips.
   - They showed understanding by explaining why they waited ("they were too hot").
   - They described the ending ("Mum came to get me").

3. **Communication Gaps:**
   - The child did not mention making or baking ingredients (flour, butter).
   - There was no description of decorating the cookies.
   - Emotional expressions and feelings about making cookies were not detailed.

4. **Emotional Understanding:**
   - While the child described actions like eating one cookie and bringing some home when Mum came to get them, they did not express their own feelings or emotions while describing these actions.

5. **Recommended Future Lesson Focus:**
   - Emphasize describing thoughts, feelings, and reactions during tasks.
   - Practice naming ingredients used in baking and talking about the sensory aspects of mixing dough and placing cookies into the oven.

6. **Suggested Reachy Prompts (simple, natural, child-friendly):**
   1. "Can you tell me what ingredients were used to make cookies?"
   2. "How did it feel when Grandma said you're good at mixing? "
   3. "What do cookies smell like when they come out of the oven?"
   4. "What does your grandma look like when she's happy and excited about baking?"
   5. "Can you remember what you ate from those cookies?"

These prompts aim to engage the child in detailed descriptions, encourage emotional expression, and help develop their sensory awareness during the process of making cookies.
