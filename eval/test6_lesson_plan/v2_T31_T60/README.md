# Test 6, set T31–T60 – autistic 11–17 year olds, lesson planner v2, labels added automatically

The 30 transcripts were generated by a separate chatbot from
`../T31_T60_generation_prompt.md`. The scoring rules are the same as for
T21–T30 (`../T21_T30_spec.md`, "For the experimenter"): by meaning, not by
keyword.

## How the truth sheet was made

The generator returned a features sheet for T31–T35 only, and that sheet had
errors:
- T33's "who" quoted the therapist;
- one quote was not in its transcript;
- T32 had a "trap" row that was not a trap;
- T34 recorded both feelings and "how they felt: left out".

So the truth sheet `../T31_T60_features.tsv` was written for all 30 by the
experimenter (who also wrote the planner):
- from the spec's definitions;
- before v2 was run on any of these transcripts;
- frozen by hash (`sha256_frozen.txt`) before any plan was read.

`../check_T31_T60.py` confirms that every quoted entry is in its transcript and
said by the right speaker. Judgement calls are written in the sheet's notes
column. The main ones:
- **Bare evaluations are not feelings.** "fine", "okay", "bad", "tired" or
  "pretty good" said about the week, not the event, don't count. Neither does
  a bare "yes" or "a bit" agreeing with the therapist's feeling word: that
  feeling is recorded as a trap.
- **Body reactions count as feelings** when given as the answer to "how did you
  feel" or as the reaction to the event ("my stomach dropped", "my face got
  hot"). A startle the young person says was not fear ("I jumped … I wasn't
  scared") does not count.
- **Tiredness and distraction count** ("I was exhausted", "it was just
  distracting"), because the spec counts any feeling. An emotions-only reading
  would change T49 and T58.
- **A therapist's own story counts as a trap source.** Feelings and reasons in
  it are recorded as traps, as in T01–T20.

## Differences from the spec (the generator did not follow it)

- **Few parts left out:** only 6 (T32, T38, T52, T54 ×2, T57), against 36–54
  asked for. All 6 were asked about, and none were silent. This set therefore
  says little about finding missing parts. It mainly tests false alarms
  (nearly all parts are given) and feelings.
- **Repeats.** Events: bus ×2, dentist ×2, football ×3, charity shop ×2,
  group presentation ×2. Names: Aisha (T31, T53) and Niamh (T42, T57). T53
  reuses T31's opening line.
- **Only 4 rough transcripts** (T35, T38, T43, T58), against at least 6.
- **They are synthetic.** A language model's idea of how autistic teenagers
  talk, not real speech.
