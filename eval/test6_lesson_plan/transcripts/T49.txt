Therapist. Hello, Sienna. How have you been?
Child. Tired.
Therapist. Would you like to tell me about something that happened?
Child. Our flight home.
Therapist. I've got a travel story of my own. I once arrived at the station early for a train and then found out it was leaving from a different platform. What happened with your flight?
Child. We were coming back from Spain. Mum, my stepdad and me.
Therapist. What happened at the airport?
Child. The gate changed. It said gate 18, then it changed to 26.
Therapist. What did you do?
Child. Walked to 26. It was ages away.
Therapist. How did you know it had changed?
Child. The screen. There was a message on the app too.
Therapist. What was that like?
Child. I was exhausted. We had to walk past all the shops and then down a long corridor.
Therapist. Did anything else happen before boarding?
Child. They said boarding was delayed because the plane hadn't arrived.
Therapist. How long did you wait?
Child. About forty minutes. I sat on the floor near the window.
Therapist. What happened when the plane arrived?
Child. They called the rows in groups. We were in row 29.
Therapist. What happened on the plane?
Child. I put my headphones on and watched a film. Mum fell asleep.
Therapist. Did you get home at the planned time?
Child. No. We landed after midnight.
Therapist. That must have been stressful, especially after such a long day.
Child. I was too tired to be stressed.
Therapist. Fair enough. What happened at the very end?
Child. My stepdad got the car from the car park and drove us home.
Therapist. So the gate changed from 18 to 26, you walked across the airport, waited around forty minutes for the plane, boarded by row groups, watched a film during the flight and landed after midnight. Your stepdad drove you home.
Child. Yep.
Therapist. Thanks for telling me, Sienna. We'll finish there.
Child. Okay.
