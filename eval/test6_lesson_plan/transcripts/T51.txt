Therapist. Hi Uma. What happened this week?
Child. I started volunteering.
Therapist. Where are you volunteering?
Child. The charity shop near the bus station.
Therapist. Who were you working with?
Child. The manager, Denise, and another volunteer called Paul.
Therapist. I've got a story from my own week. I tried to sort out a cupboard at home and found three different chargers that didn't fit anything I own. What did you do on your first day?
Child. They showed me the till and how to tag the clothes. I had to put the hangers the same way round.
Therapist. What happened when a customer came in?
Child. A woman asked if we had any blue coats. I looked through the rail but I couldn't find one.
Therapist. What did you do then?
Child. I asked Denise. She said to check the back room.
Therapist. Did you find a coat?
Child. No. There were some blue jumpers but no coats.
Therapist. How did you feel when you couldn't find it?
Child. I was worried I'd done it wrong.
Therapist. You were worried because you hadn't found the coat.
Child. Not exactly. I didn't know if I was allowed to go in the back room without asking.
Therapist. Thanks for clarifying. What happened after that?
Child. Denise showed me where the stock was kept and then I put the jumpers out.
Therapist. Did anything else happen during the shift?
Child. A man brought in a bag of books. I put them on the donations shelf.
Therapist. What happened at the end of the shift?
Child. Paul showed me how to lock the back door. Then I went home.
Therapist. So you started volunteering at the charity shop, learnt the till and clothing tags, asked Denise about the blue coat, helped with the jumpers and put donated books on the shelf. Paul showed you how to lock the back door before you went home.
Child. Yes.
Therapist. Did you enjoy it?
Child. It was okay.
Therapist. Thanks, Uma. We'll finish there.
Child. Bye.
