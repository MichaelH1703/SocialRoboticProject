1. **Narrative Summary**
   - The child described going to a big zoo in the city with their mother and brother Ben. They saw lions first, which excited them; they also encountered some snakes, making them scared due to one moving. After lunch, they visited the gift shop where the child bought a toy lion. Finally, they drove home, falling asleep in the car.

2. **Communication Strengths**
   - The child accurately described the sequence of events at the zoo and their feelings.
   - They used appropriate pronouns (e.g., "you" instead of repeating themselves).
   - They provided specific details about what happened next.

3. **Communication Gaps**
   - Limited use of conjunctions to connect thoughts (e.g., "and," "then").
   - Some redundant phrases ("I like lions") could be simplified.
   - The child did not provide a clear reason for being scared by the snake or describe their emotional progression through seeing animals.

4. **Emotional Understanding**
   - The child demonstrated an understanding of different emotions (excitement, fear).
   - They described physical reactions to their feelings (e.g., falling asleep in the car).

5. **Recommended Future Lesson Focus**
   - Continued practice with sequencing events and using conjunctions.
   - Deepening emotional vocabulary and description.
   - Developing strategies for explaining feelings more clearly.

6. **Suggested Reachy Prompts**
   - "Can you tell me what happened before lunch? What did the snakes look like?"
   - "How were you feeling after seeing the lions? Can you describe that a bit more?"
   - "What animal do you think would be most exciting to see? Why?"
