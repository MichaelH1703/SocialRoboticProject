### 1. Narrative Summary

The child described participating in a football match where their team was the Tigers. The other team scored two goals, which they contested with the referee’s decision. The child felt angry and kicked the ball away after being told off by an opponent player. They continued playing despite feeling upset.

### 2. Communication Strengths

- **Organization**: The child structured their narrative logically, starting from a general description of participating in the match to specific details like scoring and disagreements with the referee.
- **Detail Sharing**: The child provided detailed information about the goals scored by their team (the Tigers) and their opponents, as well as specific events like being in goal and getting told off after kicking away the ball.

### 3. Communication Gaps

- **Specificity on Event Sequence**: The child did not clearly state what happened immediately after the referee gave a goal that was contested.
- **Use of Pronouns**: Some instances lacked clear use of pronouns (e.g., "they" vs. specific names like "the Tigers"), which could be confusing for listeners.
- **Inconsistent Memory Recall**: There were moments where the child couldn't remember details about the match, such as who was watching or where it took place.

### 4. Emotional Understanding

The child demonstrated an understanding of their emotions by describing feeling angry and frustrated ("Really angry") after being told off during the game. However, they avoided elaborating on this emotion throughout their description, suggesting a need for further work in expressing feelings more fully.

### 5. Recommended Future Lesson Focus

- **Sequence of Events**: Reinforce skills related to clearly describing what happened in chronological order.
- **Pronoun Usage**: Teach proper use and clarity with pronouns to enhance understanding and communication clarity.
- **Emotional Expression**: Work on developing the child's ability to express emotions more fully, including providing reasons for their feelings.

### 6. Suggested Reachy Prompts (simple, natural, child-friendly)

1. **Describe what you did after getting told off in your match.**
2. **Tell me who played against you during your football game?**
3. **Can you say how you felt when the referee gave a goal?**
4. **Where were you and where was your family during the game?**
5. **Who did you score a goal for during the match?**
6. **How many goals did your team score in total?**

These prompts aim to address specific areas of improvement, whether they be sequence clarity, pronoun usage, or emotional expression, ensuring both future therapy sessions and robot interactions are tailored appropriately.
