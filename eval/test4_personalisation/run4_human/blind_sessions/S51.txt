Lesson focus:
  The focus should be on teaching empathy and perspective-taking by helping April understand other people’s feelings better and how her actions can affect them.

Profile style checklist:
  1. Start from something she likes.
  2. Give specific praise, not general praise.
  3. Put a comment between questions; not many questions in a row.
