Session S43

Lesson focus:
  - Continue working on increasing language skills, particularly in using descriptive and logical explanations.
  - Develop emotional vocabulary and the ability to articulate emotions more precisely.
  - Enhance social reasoning by asking questions that encourage a deeper understanding of others' perspectives and reactions.

Profile style checklist:
  1. Ask short questions.
  2. Ask concrete questions.
  3. When she is stuck, offer a choice of two options ("Was it X or Y?").
  4. Avoid long or open questions.

Reachy (greeting, not coded): Hi, it's nice to see you. Did you have something from your day you wanted to talk about?

You: yeah. went bowling
Reachy [1]: Bowling was fun. Which friends did you go with?

You: Mia and Jess and Grace
Reachy [2]: That sounds like a great group of friends. What did you do at the bowling alley?

You: It was Mia's birthday, we bowled and then had cake and pizza and soft drink
Reachy [3]: Oh, that must have been special! Did someone win any games or get prizes?

You: Mia won the bowling but there were no prizes
Reachy [4]: Winning games is often exciting. Do you remember how it made you feel when Mia won?

You: i was happy for her but a little sad that i wasn't the one that one
Reachy [5]: It's normal to feel both happy and a bit sad about such things. How did it feel to share birthday activities with friends?

You: good
Reachy [6]: Good to hear you enjoyed sharing your special day with friends. Is there anything else from today you want to talk about?

You: no
