Session S66

Lesson focus:
  - Continue working on increasing language skills, particularly in using descriptive and logical explanations.
  - Develop emotional vocabulary and the ability to articulate emotions more precisely.
  - Enhance social reasoning by asking questions that encourage a deeper understanding of others' perspectives and reactions.

Profile style checklist:
  1. Ask short questions.
  2. Ask concrete questions.
  3. When she is stuck, offer a choice of two options ("Was it X or Y?").
  4. Avoid long or open questions.

Reachy (greeting, not coded): Hi, it's nice to see you. Did you have something from your day you wanted to talk about?

You: yeah. i went bowling
Reachy [1]: That sounds fun. Who was there with you when you went bowling?

You: I was there for Mia's birthday party
Reachy [2]: Oh, that makes it even more special. How did it feel going to the bowling place on such a nice day?

You: yeah it felt very good
Reachy [3]: It felt very good indeed. What games did you play at the bowling alley?

You: we just did bowling
Reachy [4]: That sounds simple but fun. How many people were there with you at the bowling alley?

You: it was me and mia and grace and jess and mia's mum and dad were there too. but they didn't bowl, only me and grace and jess and mia bowled
Reachy [5]: Nice to know more friends joined in the fun. How did it feel playing bowling with your friends?

You: yeah it felt really good, it was alot of fun
Reachy [6]: It sounds like a wonderful time you had! Was there anything Mia's mum or dad did at the bowling alley?

You: they bought us all pizza and then Mia's mum had made a cake
Reachy [7]: That's nice to have. Who ate the pizza and cake after bowling?

You: i dont know
Reachy [8]: Oh, I see. Did you eat with anyone in particular at the party?

You: yeah we all ate together
Reachy [9]: All of you had a good time together eating the pizza and cake. Did either of the adults join in your bowling games?

You: no
Reachy [10]: That's okay, it sounds like Mia's mum and dad enjoyed watching everyone have fun instead. How did Mia feel about bowling on her birthday?

You: she was happy i think
Reachy [11]: Mia was definitely happy with the bowling party on her special day. Thank you for sharing that with me.
