Session S74

Lesson focus:
  - Improve narrative sequencing by clarifying which event happened first.
  - Enhance specific detail recall for personal actions and emotional responses during games.
  - Develop understanding of different types of emotions related to sports outcomes (e.g., joy, disappointment).

Profile style checklist:
  1. No jokes, sarcasm or figures of speech.
  2. One question at a time.
  3. Say what is coming next.
  4. Ask what happened before asking about feelings.

Reachy (greeting, not coded): Hi, it's nice to see you. Did you have something from your day you wanted to talk about?

You: yeah i went bowling today
Reachy [1]: That sounds fun. What did you do at the bowling alley?

You: we had a party, it was Mia's birthday
Reachy [2]: Oh really?

You: yes
Reachy [3]: Who else from your life was there?

You: my friends Jess and Grace were there as well
Reachy [4]: Got it. And how did you feel at the birthday party?

You: it was fun
Reachy [5]: That's great to hear! How did they make the party fun for you?

You: what?
Reachy [6]: How did Jess and Grace make the party enjoyable for you?

You: i dont know
Reachy [7]: Can you remember what games or activities you played at the bowling alley?

You: yeah we did bowling and we played ski ball as well
Reachy [8]: What happened next after playing those games?

You: we had pizza and birthday cake
Reachy [9]: Sounds delicious! And then what did you do?

You: Mia's mum drove me home
Reachy [10]: And how did you feel when you went home?

You: tired
Reachy [11]: That’s understandable. Did anyone in your family come to pick you up?

You: no, Mia's mum drove me home
Reachy [12]: Got it. And that made you tired as well. Thank you for sharing about your day!

You: goodbye
Reachy (scripted, not coded): Thanks for talking with me today, April. Is there anything more you wanted to talk about?

You: no
Reachy (scripted, not coded): Okay. Thank you for sharing your day with me, April. Goodbye.
