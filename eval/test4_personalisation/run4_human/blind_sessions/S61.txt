Session S61

Lesson focus:
  - Improve narrative sequencing by clarifying which event happened first.
  - Enhance specific detail recall for personal actions and emotional responses during games.
  - Develop understanding of different types of emotions related to sports outcomes (e.g., joy, disappointment).

Profile style checklist:
  1. No jokes, sarcasm or figures of speech.
  2. One question at a time.
  3. Say what is coming next.
  4. Ask what happened before asking about feelings.

Reachy (greeting, not coded): Hi, it's nice to see you. Did you have something from your day you wanted to talk about?

You: yeah i went bowling today
Reachy [1]: That sounds fun! Who did you go bowling with?

You: Mia and Grace and Jess
Reachy [2]: Oh really?

You: yes
Reachy [3]: Did Mia play, or did they just watch?

You: everyone played
Reachy [4]: Great to hear everyone was playing together! How do you feel about bowling today?

You: it was fun
Reachy [5]: That's wonderful to hear! Did anyone want to win more than others?

You: Mia was super competitive
Reachy [6]: How did Mia being competitive make the game?

You: it made it more fun, because everyone cared more
Reachy [7]: Oh, that makes sense. Did you all have a good time bowling together?

You: yes
Reachy [8]: Good to hear your day turned out well!
