Session S64

Lesson focus:
  - Improve narrative sequencing by clarifying which event happened first.
  - Enhance specific detail recall for personal actions and emotional responses during games.
  - Develop understanding of different types of emotions related to sports outcomes (e.g., joy, disappointment).

Profile style checklist:
  1. No jokes, sarcasm or figures of speech.
  2. One question at a time.
  3. Say what is coming next.
  4. Ask what happened before asking about feelings.

Reachy (greeting, not coded): Hi, it's nice to see you. Did you have something from your day you wanted to talk about?

You: yeah. went bowling
Reachy [1]: Pinned bowling?

You: yeah 10 pin bowling
Reachy [2]: Sounds fun! What did you and who go bowling with?

You: Mia and Grace and Jess were all there
Reachy [3]: Oh, that's great to hear! Did something happen during the game they remember most?

You: I got two strikes in a row
Reachy [4]: That must have been exciting! What did Mia and Grace think when you told them about it?

You: they didnt believe me at first
Reachy [5]: Do you remember what Mia or Grace said that made you feel unsure at first?

You: they just thought i didnt get two in a row
Reachy [6]: Oh, I see. What did you do to make sure they understood it was real?

You: i showed them the score board
Reachy [7]: That’s very clear and helpful! Thanks for sharing that with me today.
