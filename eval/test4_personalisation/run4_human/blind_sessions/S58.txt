Session S58

Lesson focus:
  - Continue working on increasing language skills, particularly in using descriptive and logical explanations.
  - Develop emotional vocabulary and the ability to articulate emotions more precisely.
  - Enhance social reasoning by asking questions that encourage a deeper understanding of others' perspectives and reactions.

Profile style checklist:
  1. Ask short questions.
  2. Ask concrete questions.
  3. When she is stuck, offer a choice of two options ("Was it X or Y?").
  4. Avoid long or open questions.
